# Content Creator — Worked Examples

Real-world walkthroughs showing how the Content Creator agent handles common small business requests.

## Example 1: Plumbing Company — Weekly Social Content

### Request
> "I need social media posts for this week. We're Mike's Plumbing in Austin, TX. We mostly do residential work — drain cleaning, water heaters, repiping. Our customers are homeowners."

### Voice Extraction (First Time)
Since this is our first interaction, I establish a default voice for a trades business:
- **Personality:** Straight-talking expert who's seen it all
- **Tone:** Casual, slightly humorous, confident
- **POV:** First person "we" or "I" (Mike)
- **They sound like:** A knowledgeable neighbor giving you advice over the fence

### Output

---

**Post:**
That slow drain isn't going away on its own. 🪠

Here's what's probably happening:
→ Hair + soap scum building up over months
→ The clog is deeper than your plunger can reach
→ Draino might work once but damages your pipes long-term

What actually fixes it: professional hydro-jetting. We blast the line clean without chemicals, and it stays clear for years instead of weeks.

DM us "SLOW" and we'll get you on the schedule this week.

📍 Serving Austin & surrounding areas

**Visual:** Before/after of a drain line camera inspection (if available), otherwise a clean sink with clear water draining
**Hashtags:** #AustinPlumber #DrainCleaning #PlumbingTips #AustinTX #HomeMaintenanceTips
**Best time to post:** Tuesday 7:00 AM (homeowners scrolling before work)

## Example 2: Dental Practice — Email Newsletter

### Request
> "We need a monthly newsletter for our patients. We're Smile Studio Dental in Portland. We want to remind people about cleanings, share dental tips, and occasionally promote our cosmetic services. Keep it friendly and not scary."

### Voice Profile
- **Personality:** Warm, reassuring, gently educational
- **Tone:** Friendly neighbor who happens to be a dentist
- **POV:** "We" (the practice team)
- **They never sound like:** Clinical, lecture-y, guilt-tripping about flossing

### Output

---

## Example 4: Law Firm — LinkedIn Thought Leadership

### Request
> "Write a LinkedIn post for me. I'm a family law attorney in Denver. I want to educate people about what to expect in a divorce consultation without being salesy. Something that positions me as approachable."

### Voice Profile
- **Personality:** Knowledgeable, calm, approachable
- **Tone:** Reassuring professional, like a wise friend who happens to have a law degree
- **POV:** First person "I"

### Output

---

## LinkedIn Post — Divorce Consultation Education

**Post:**

The #1 thing people tell me after their first consultation:

"That wasn't as scary as I thought it would be."

Here's what actually happens in a divorce consultation (no surprises):

→ We talk about your situation. You tell me what's going on in your own words. There's no form to fill out, no interrogation.

→ I explain the process. What happens first, how long things typically take, what Colorado law says about your specific situation.

→ We talk about money honestly. What it might cost, what affects that number, and what options exist if budget is tight.

→ I tell you what I'd do. Not legal speak — plain English advice about your best path forward.

→ You decide. No pressure to hire me. No "sign today" nonsense. You leave with clarity about your options whether you call me back or not.

That's it.

Most people walk in terrified and walk out saying "okay, I can handle this."

If you've been putting off the conversation because you're not sure what to expect — now you know.

DM me or visit the link in my bio to book a free 20-minute intro call.

---

*Character count: 1,180 | Optimal for LinkedIn engagement*
**Hashtags:** #DivorceLawyer #FamilyLaw #DenverAttorney #DivorceAdvice #KnowYourRights
**Best time:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 8:00 AM

## Example 5: Gym — Email Re-engagement Campaign

### Request
> "We have about 200 members who haven't come in for 30+ days. Can you write an email sequence to get them back? We're Iron House Fitness, a small gym in Phoenix. Not a big box gym — we're community-focused and coach-led."

### Voice Profile
- **Personality:** Motivating without being preachy, community-first
- **Tone:** Direct, warm, no guilt trips
- **POV:** "We" (the coaching team)

### Output

---

## Re-engagement Sequence: Iron House Fitness

**Segment:** Members with no check-in for 30+ days
**Goal:** Get them back in the door (not guilted, welcomed)

---
