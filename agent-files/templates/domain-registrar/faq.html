Domains

A domain name is your unique address on the internet — like yourbusiness.com. It points to your website and email and is how people find you online.

Domain registration is instant. As soon as payment is confirmed your domain is registered and DNS is propagating globally. Most updates are live within 60 seconds on our network.

Yes. You can transfer any domain currently registered at another registrar to Void Domains. Transfers include a 1-year renewal at no extra charge. The process typically takes 5-7 days and requires an authorization code from your current registrar.

We send renewal notices at 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry. If auto-renew is enabled your domain renews automatically. After expiry there is a 30-day grace period where you can still renew at standard pricing, followed by a 30-day redemption period at a higher fee. After that the domain is released to the public.

Yes — WHOIS privacy protection is included on every domain at no extra cost. Your personal name, address, email, and phone are replaced with our proxy information in the public WHOIS database.

Pricing & Billing

No. The price shown is the price you pay — registration, renewal, and WHOIS privacy all included. ICANN charges a small per-year fee ($0.18) that is included in our listed prices.

We accept all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and select cryptocurrencies via our payment processor. All transactions are secured with TLS encryption.

Domain registrations can be refunded within 5 days of purchase for .com, .net, .org, and most standard TLDs. Premium TLDs and certain ccTLDs may be non-refundable. Site template purchases are non-refundable once the AI setup process has begun.

Site Launch Service

After purchasing a Launch or Pro plan you are connected with an AI assistant that walks you through selecting a template, choosing a theme, and entering your business information. The agent deploys the site to your domain within minutes. You receive a fully functional website — no coding required.

The AI assistant handles minor customizations — text content, color theme, contact information, and page selection. Full rebrand or custom design work is outside the scope of the service. The templates are clean, standards-compliant HTML/CSS and can be further developed by any web developer.

Currently available: Landing Page, Blog, Small Business, Company Profile, Product + Pricing, and Basic Ecommerce with Stripe and PayPal checkout. Domain Registrar template is also available. All templates come in 10 style variants across 2 design systems (Brutalist Glass and Neo-Minimal) with 5 color themes each.

Launch plan includes 1 year of static site hosting on our global CDN. Pro plan includes 2 years. After the included period, hosting renews at $5/month. You can also export your site files and host anywhere.
