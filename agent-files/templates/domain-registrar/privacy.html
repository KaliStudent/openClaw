Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Void Domains ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website, services, and products.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account registration information (name, email address, password)
  • Billing information (credit card details processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Domain registration information required by ICANN (name, address, email, phone — protected by WHOIS privacy on all eligible domains)
  • Communications you send to our support team

Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookie Policy below)
  • DNS query data for domains managed through our nameservers (aggregated, not personally identifiable)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process domain registrations, renewals, and transfers
  • Bill you for services purchased
  • Send renewal reminders, service notifications, and security alerts
  • Provide customer support and operate the AI site launch service
  • Comply with ICANN requirements and applicable laws
  • Improve our services through aggregated analytics

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. WHOIS Privacy

For eligible domains, we automatically substitute our proxy contact information in the public WHOIS database in place of your personal information. Your actual information is stored securely and disclosed only when required by law or ICANN dispute resolution procedures.

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Domain registries — required by ICANN to process registrations
  • Payment processors — to process transactions securely
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting and DNS services under data processing agreements
  • Law enforcement — when required by valid legal process

5. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active and for a period after closure as required by law or legitimate business needs. Domain registration data may be retained as required by ICANN agreements.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption, two-factor authentication options, and regular security audits. No system is completely secure; we encourage you to use a strong unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@void.domains.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management, and optional analytics cookies to understand how our service is used. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@void.domains or use our contact form.
