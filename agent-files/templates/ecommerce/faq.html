Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about orders, shipping, returns, and payments.

Orders & Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Expedited (2-day) and overnight options are available at checkout. Orders placed before 2pm EST ship same day.

Yes — we ship to 40+ countries. International orders typically arrive in 7–14 business days. Duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

You'll receive a tracking number via email when your order ships. You can also log into your account to view order status at any time.

Returns & Refunds

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. To start a return, contact our support team with your order number.

Refunds are processed within 2 business days of receiving the returned item. Credit card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. PayPal refunds are usually instant.

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the damage. We'll ship a replacement immediately at no charge — no need to return the damaged item.

Payments

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), and PayPal. All transactions are SSL encrypted.

Yes. We use Stripe and PayPal for all payment processing — your card details are never stored on our servers. Both providers are PCI-DSS compliant.

We support buy-now-pay-later options (Klarna, Afterpay) on orders over $50. These options will appear at checkout if eligible for your region.
