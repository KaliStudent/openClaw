About

The Signal is a publication about technology, design, and building things that matter.

We write for builders — engineers, designers, founders, and anyone who cares about the craft of making software. No listicles. No clickbait. No sponsored content.

The Signal started in 2024 as a weekly newsletter. It became a publication when the archives outgrew the inbox. Every article is written to last — we aim for pieces you'll still be sending people in three years.

We cover CSS architecture, AI systems, product strategy, design philosophy, and the occasional deep dive into something obscure and useful. If that sounds like your thing, subscribe.
