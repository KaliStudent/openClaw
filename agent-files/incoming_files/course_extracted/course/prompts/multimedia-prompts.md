# Multimedia Asset Prompts
## Reading the Digital World: Understanding Icons & Symbols Online

All prompts below are written for an image or video generation LLM (e.g., Midjourney, DALL·E, Sora, RunwayML). Each prompt is labeled with its asset ID, used in the corresponding lesson file.

## MODULE 1 — Welcome

### M1L1-IMG-001
**Lesson:** Module 1, Lesson 1 — What Are Icons?
**Type:** Illustration
**Prompt:**
> A friendly, clean illustration showing two signs side by side. On the left: a traditional green road sign with white text that says "CAUTION — ROAD WORK AHEAD." On the right: the universally recognized orange construction worker icon (a simple silhouette figure with a shovel). The icons should be large, clearly visible, and centered on a warm white background. Below each sign, a small label reads "Words" and "Icon." Style: flat vector illustration, accessible and friendly, suitable for adult learners. No photographic realism.

## Notes for the Multimedia Producer

1. All videos should have **captions/subtitles** — many learners in this audience have hearing difficulties.
2. Minimum font size in any video or image should be **16pt / 22px equivalent** for readability.
3. Use the course color palette where appropriate: **Primary Blue #2563eb**, **Light Blue #dbeafe**, **Warm White #f8fafc**, **Accent Teal #0d9488**.
4. Avoid stock photos of young models — use illustrations or photos of adults aged 55–80 where humans are depicted.
5. Pacing for all videos: **unhurried**. Assume viewers may need 2–3x longer than typical to process each step.
6. Prefer **flat illustration** over photorealism for icons and UI diagrams.
