Module 7

AI Chat & Feedback Icons

⏱ 15 minutes 📘 Beginner Lesson 11 of 20

What You Will Learn

  • Understand what AI chat tools like Claude and ChatGPT are and why they exist
  • Recognize the icons inside AI chat interfaces
  • Know how to give feedback, copy text, regenerate answers, and start fresh
  • Understand what the thumbs up and thumbs down icons actually do

What Is an AI Chat Tool?

In recent years, a new kind of tool has appeared on the internet: AI chat tools. These are programs you can ask questions, request help writing letters, get explanations of confusing topics, or simply have a conversation with. They respond in plain written language, like a knowledgeable friend who is always available.

The most widely used AI chat tools right now are:

  • Claude (claude.ai) — made by a company called Anthropic
  • ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) — made by a company called OpenAI
  • Gemini (gemini.google.com) — made by Google

They all look similar and use many of the same icons. Once you learn the icons in one, you can use all of them.

The Chat Interface — What You Are Looking At

When you open an AI chat tool, you see a simple screen. Usually there is a white or dark background with a text box at the bottom where you type your question. The conversation history appears above it — your messages on one side (often in a colored bubble), the AI's responses on the other (usually in plain text).

After each AI response, a small row of tiny icons appears. These are feedback and utility icons — they let you rate, copy, or change the response.

Icons in Claude (claude.ai)

👍
Thumbs Up
Tell Claude the response was helpful or accurate. This feedback trains the AI to give better answers to you and everyone else over time. You are helping improve the tool.
👎
Thumbs Down
Tell Claude the response was unhelpful, inaccurate, or off-topic. A small form may ask why. This feedback is valuable — do not hesitate to use it.
📋
Copy
Copies the entire response text to your clipboard. Then you can paste it into an email, a Word document, or a text message. Very useful for saving AI-written content.
🔄
Regenerate / Retry
Ask Claude to write a new response to your same question. Useful when the first answer was not quite right — you get a second attempt without retyping.
✏️
Edit Message
Go back and change the question you originally asked. When you save your edit, Claude generates a fresh answer to the updated question.
➕
New Chat
Start a brand new conversation. Claude will not remember anything from your previous chats when you start fresh. Good for switching to a completely different topic.

Icons in ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)

ChatGPT uses nearly identical icons with the same purposes:

IconNameWhat It Does
👍 Good Response Rate the response positively. Same as Claude's thumbs up.
👎 Bad Response Rate the response negatively. Optionally explain why.
📋 Copy Copy the response text to paste elsewhere.
🔄 Regenerate Ask ChatGPT for a new attempt at answering the same question.
🔊 Read Aloud ChatGPT reads the response out loud in a natural-sounding voice. Great if reading is difficult.
⋯ (three dots) More Options Opens a small menu with additional actions like "Share" or "Report."

The Text Input Bar — Where You Type

At the bottom of every AI chat interface is the text input bar. Inside or beside it you will usually find:

📎
Attach File
Upload a photo, document, or PDF. You can ask the AI to read and summarize the file, answer questions about it, or help you edit it.
🎙
Voice Input
Speak your question instead of typing. The AI transcribes your speech into text and responds. Excellent for anyone who finds typing difficult.
↑ Arrow
Send
An upward-pointing arrow button. Press this (or press Enter on a keyboard) to send your message to the AI. It turns active once you have typed something.
⏹
Stop
A square "stop" icon that appears while the AI is generating a response. Press it to interrupt the AI mid-response if you realize you asked the wrong question.

Things AI Chat Cannot Do — And the Icons That Show Limits

Sometimes the AI will respond with a disclaimer or a note. Here are a few icons and labels you might see that indicate a limitation:

  • ⚠ Warning symbol: The AI is uncertain about something and flagging that you should verify the information with another source.
  • "Web search" icon (globe): Some AI tools can browse the internet for up-to-date information. A globe icon indicates the AI searched the web to answer your question.
  • Lock 🔒: A feature requires a paid subscription to use. You can still use the free version — the lock just means certain extras cost money.
Important: AI chat tools are very helpful but they can sometimes be wrong. Treat their answers the way you would treat advice from a knowledgeable friend — useful as a starting point, but for medical, legal, or financial decisions, always follow up with a licensed professional.
