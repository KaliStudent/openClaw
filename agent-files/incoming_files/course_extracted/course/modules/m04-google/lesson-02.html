Module 4

Google App Icons — Gmail, Maps, Drive & More

⏱ 20 minutes 📘 Beginner Lesson 5 of 20

What You Will Learn

  • Identify the icon for each major Google app by color and shape
  • Understand what Gmail, Google Maps, Google Drive, Google Photos, Chrome, and YouTube each do
  • Recognize the icons inside those apps (compose, navigate, upload, share)
  • Know that all these apps connect to your single Google account

Why Google Has So Many Apps

Google started as a search engine, but over the years it has built a whole family of apps that all work together. Because they share one account — your Google Account — your photos, emails, documents, and search history are all connected. You sign in once and everything is there.

Each app has its own colorful icon so you can spot it quickly on your phone's home screen. Let's go through the most important ones.

Remember: You do not have to use all of these. Start with one or two that solve a problem you have right now, and explore the others gradually.

The Big Six Google Apps

📧
Gmail
Google's free email service. The icon is a colorful letter "M" that looks like an envelope flap. Red, blue, green, and yellow.
🗺
Google Maps
Turn-by-turn directions, business search, street view. The icon is a teardrop-shaped map pin in red and white.
💾
Google Drive
Free online storage for documents, photos, and files. The icon is a triangle with blue, green, and yellow sides — like a pinwheel or folder.
📸
Google Photos
Stores and organizes all your photos and videos automatically. The icon is a pinwheel of four rounded petals — blue, red, yellow, green.
🌐
Chrome
Google's web browser — used to visit websites. The icon is a circle divided into red, yellow, and green thirds, with a blue dot in the center.
▶️
YouTube
Video watching and sharing. The icon is a red rectangle with a white triangle "play" button in the middle.

Icons Inside Gmail

Once you open Gmail, you will see a long list of emails. These are your messages. Along the top and bottom of the screen are small icons. Here are the most important:

IconNameWhat It Does
✏️ (pencil) Compose Start writing a brand new email to someone. This is the most-used icon in Gmail.
↩️ (left arrow) Reply Send an answer back to the person who emailed you. Only goes back to them.
↩️↩️ (double arrow) Reply All Reply to everyone who received the original email — use carefully if there is a large group.
→ (right arrow) Forward Send the email you received to a different person.
🗑 (trash can) Delete Move the email to the Trash. It is not permanently gone for 30 days.
⚐ (flag) Star / Flag Mark an email as important so you can find it easily later. Starred emails glow gold.
📎 (paperclip) Attachment A file (photo, document, PDF) is included in the email. Tap it to open or save the file.
⊘ (circle with line) Block/Spam Report the email as junk. Gmail will stop showing you messages from that sender.

Icons Inside Google Maps

Google Maps is one of the most useful apps on any phone. Once you search for a place or get directions, you will see these icons:

IconNameWhat It Does
📍 (pin) Location Marker A red teardrop marking a specific place on the map. Tap it to see the name and address.
➡ (blue arrow) Directions Start turn-by-turn navigation to a destination. Tap this after you find a place.
🎯 (crosshair / dot) Your Location A blue dot shows exactly where you are on the map right now. It moves as you move.
🧭 (compass) Compass / Orientation Rotates the map to match the direction you are facing. Tap to re-center.
🚶 🚗 🚌 Travel Mode Switch between walking, driving, transit, or cycling directions.
⭐ (star) Save Place Save a location to your favorites so you can find it again quickly.
👁 (eye / person on street) Street View See a real photo of what the street actually looks like. Drag the little yellow person icon onto any road to enter Street View.

Icons Inside Google Drive & Google Photos

+
New / Upload
In Drive: Create a new document, spreadsheet, or folder. Also lets you upload a file from your device.
↗
Share
In Drive: Share a file with another person by sending them a link or inviting their email address.
⬇
Download
Save a copy of a file from Drive to your device. Useful before traveling somewhere without internet.
🔒
Private / Lock
In Photos: Moves sensitive pictures into a locked folder that requires your fingerprint or PIN to view.

One Account, Everything Connected

Here is the most important thing to understand about Google apps: they all use your one Google Account. This means:

  • Photos you take on your phone automatically appear in Google Photos (if you have backup turned on)
  • Emails you receive in Gmail are available on your phone, computer, and tablet simultaneously
  • Documents saved in Google Drive can be opened on any device by signing in
  • Directions saved in Google Maps are remembered across devices

This is called cloud storage. Your files live on Google's computers, accessible wherever you are.

Tip: If you ever get a new phone, signing into your Google account immediately restores your photos, contacts, apps, and settings. You do not lose anything.
