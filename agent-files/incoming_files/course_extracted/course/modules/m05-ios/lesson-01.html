Module 5

The iPhone Status Bar

⏱ 15 minutes 📘 Beginner Lesson 6 of 20

What You Will Learn

  • Read every icon in the narrow bar at the very top of your iPhone screen
  • Know your battery level, signal strength, and Wi-Fi status at a glance
  • Understand what the small colored dots and lines in the corner mean
  • Recognize when your phone is in a special mode (airplane, Do Not Disturb, focus)

What Is the Status Bar?

Look at the very top of your iPhone screen right now. That thin strip — above all your apps — is called the status bar. It is always there, always updating, and full of useful information. Most people glance at it dozens of times a day without really knowing what they are seeing.

On newer iPhones (iPhone X and later), the top corners have a small notch or a little pill-shaped cutout called the Dynamic Island. The time lives in the top left corner; battery and signal icons live in the top right corner. On older iPhones, these icons stretch all the way across in one bar.

Signal & Connectivity Icons

📶
Cellular Signal
Four or five vertical bars. More filled bars = stronger signal. No bars = no signal. You may see 5G, LTE, or 4G beside the bars — these indicate the speed of your cellular connection.
🛜
Wi-Fi
A dot with curved lines above it (like a fan). The more curves showing, the stronger your Wi-Fi connection. If Wi-Fi is off or you are not connected, this icon disappears.
✈
Airplane Mode
When active, all wireless connections (cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth) are turned off. Required on airplanes during flight. Your phone still works for offline apps and games.
🔵
Bluetooth
A small stylized "B" shape. Appears when Bluetooth is turned on and your phone is connected to a wireless device like earbuds or a speaker.

Battery Icons

🔋
Battery Level
A small rectangle that fills with green, yellow, or red depending on how much charge remains. Green = good (above 20%). Yellow = Low Power Mode. Red = critically low (below 10%).
⚡
Charging
A small lightning bolt appears inside or beside the battery icon when your phone is plugged in and charging.
🔆
Low Power Mode
Battery icon turns yellow. Your phone has automatically reduced background activity to save battery life. You can turn this on manually in Settings to extend battery when traveling.
Tip: If you want to always see your exact battery percentage as a number, go to Settings → Battery → Battery Percentage and toggle it on. Then you will see "87%" next to the battery icon instead of guessing from the fill level.

Do Not Disturb & Focus Mode Icons

iPhones have a system called Focus that silences notifications during certain times or activities. When a Focus mode is active, you will see a small icon in the status bar.

IconWhat It MeansHow to Turn It Off
🌙 Moon Do Not Disturb is on. Calls and notifications are silenced. People who call will get your voicemail. Swipe down from the top right corner → tap the moon icon to toggle off.
🏠 House Personal Focus is on — a quiet mode meant for relaxing at home. Same as above — Control Center, then tap the active Focus mode to end it.
💼 Briefcase Work Focus — silences personal apps during work hours. Control Center toggle or Settings → Focus.
😴 Zzz Sleep Focus is on — your phone is in overnight quiet mode. Will automatically turn off at your scheduled wake time, or override in Control Center.

The Privacy Dots — A Very Important Safety Feature

Apple added two small colored dots to iPhones as a privacy protection. They appear in the top-right corner of the screen, beside the battery icon.

🟠
Orange Dot
Your microphone is currently being used by an app. This appears during phone calls, voice recordings, or when Siri is listening. If you see this when you are not on a call or using voice commands, check which app is open.
🟢
Green Dot
Your camera is currently being used. Appears during video calls, taking photos, or video recording. If you see this unexpectedly, swipe up to check which app is active.
Safety Alert: The orange and green dots are Apple's way of keeping you informed. No app can secretly use your microphone or camera without triggering these dots. If you see them when you did not expect them, swipe down the Control Center (top right corner) — it shows you which app triggered the dot.

Other Status Bar Icons You May See

IconNameWhat It Means
📍 Arrow Location Services An app is currently using your GPS location (Maps, weather app, etc.). A hollow arrow means an app recently used location. Solid arrow = using it right now.
🔄 Spinning circle Activity / Loading Your phone is downloading something or processing a task in the background.
🔒 Lock Portrait Lock Your screen will not rotate sideways when you tilt the phone. Useful when reading in bed. Toggle in Control Center.
⏰ Clock Alarm Set You have an alarm scheduled. Appears as a small clock icon so you know without opening the Clock app.
