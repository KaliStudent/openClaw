Module 5

iPhone Built-In App Icons

⏱ 15 minutes 📘 Beginner Lesson 8 of 20

What You Will Learn

  • Identify every pre-installed Apple app by its icon color and symbol
  • Know at a glance what each app is for
  • Understand the icons inside Messages, FaceTime, and the App Store
  • Feel confident opening the right app for the task

Apps That Come with Every iPhone

Apple installs a set of apps on every iPhone before you even turn it on. These are called built-in apps. You cannot delete most of them, but you do not have to use them. Learning their icons means you will always know where to find the right tool for a task.

📱
Phone
Green background with a white handset icon. Makes and receives phone calls. Also stores your recent calls and voicemail.
💬
Messages
Green background with a white speech bubble. Send and receive text messages and iMessages (internet messages to other Apple users).
📹
FaceTime
Green background with a white video camera. Free video and audio calls to other Apple devices. Like a phone call — but you can see each other.
📷
Camera
Dark gray background with a camera lens icon. Takes photos and videos. One of the most-used apps on any phone.
🌅
Photos
White background with a colorful pinwheel flower. This is your photo album — every photo and video you take is stored here automatically.
🗺
Maps
White background with a road map illustration. Apple's navigation app. Works like Google Maps for directions and finding nearby places.
🌐
Safari
Blue background with a compass needle. Apple's web browser — opens websites. The blue compass is Safari's signature icon.
📧
Mail
Blue background with a white envelope. Apple's built-in email app. You can add any email account (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) to it.
⚙️
Settings
Gray background with a gear icon. Where you control everything on your phone — privacy, display brightness, sounds, passwords, and more.
🛍
App Store
Blue background with a white letter "A" made of a pencil and ruler. Download free and paid apps to your phone here.
🎵
Music
Dark background with a colorful music note. Apple's music player — plays songs from your library or from Apple Music (subscription required).
🗓
Calendar
White background showing today's date in large numbers. Schedule appointments, set reminders, and see upcoming events.
🖼

Icons Inside Messages

When you open the Messages app, you will see a list of conversations. Tap any conversation to open it. Inside a conversation you will find these icons:

IconWhat It Does
📎 Plus (+) button Attach a photo, video, file, or use features like location sharing or the camera directly from the conversation.
🎙 Microphone Hold to record and send a voice message instead of typing.
↑ Arrow (send button) A blue upward arrow — taps sends your message. It turns blue once you have typed something.
💙 Blue speech bubble An iMessage — sent over internet to another iPhone or Apple device. Free to send, even internationally.
💚 Green speech bubble A standard text message (SMS) sent over your cellular network. Counts against your text plan if you have one.

Understanding the App Store

The App Store is where you go to add new capabilities to your iPhone. Every app you download comes from here. The store has icons of its own to help you navigate:

☁ ⬇
Download / Cloud with Arrow
Tap this to download an app. If you have downloaded it before, a cloud icon with a down arrow lets you re-install it for free.
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Star Ratings
Other users' ratings out of 5 stars. Look for apps with 4+ stars and many reviews before downloading.
🔄
Update
An app has a newer version available. Tapping Update installs improvements and security fixes. Keeping apps updated is a good habit.
Safety Tip: Only download apps from the official App Store. Apple reviews every app before it is listed. Never download apps from websites or links in text messages — these can be scams or harmful software.

Quick Check
