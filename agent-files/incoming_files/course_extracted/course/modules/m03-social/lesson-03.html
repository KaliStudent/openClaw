X (Twitter) Icons

⏱ 12 min read🎯 Beginner📄 Lesson 3 of 4

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What X (formerly Twitter) is and how it works
  • The meaning of Reply, Repost, Like, Bookmark, and Share
  • What a verified checkmark means on X
  • What the "For You" and "Following" tabs mean
ℹ️

Twitter is Now Called X

The platform was called "Twitter" for many years. In 2023, it was renamed "X" by its new owner. Many people still call it Twitter — both names refer to the same platform. On this platform, posts are called "posts" (previously called "tweets").

📱

What Is X / Twitter?

X is a platform for short public posts — text, photos, and videos. People use it to share opinions, follow news, discuss topics, and interact with public figures, celebrities, journalists, and politicians. Posts have a character limit, so people write concisely.

Icons Under Every Post on X

Under each post on X, there is a row of five icons. Here is what each one does:

IconNameWhat It Does
💬Reply (Speech Bubble)Write a public response to this post. Everyone can see your reply. It's like a public conversation.
🔁Repost (Two Arrows)Re-shares the post to your own followers. Like forwarding an email but publicly. If you hold the button, you can add your own comment to it (called a "Quote Post").
🤍Like (Heart)Shows you appreciate the post. The heart turns red when you like it. Tap again to unlike.
🔖BookmarkSaves this post to your private bookmarks so you can find it again later.
⬆️ShareCopy a link to the post, share it to another app, or send it as a direct message.

The Number Counter Next to Each Icon

Next to each icon you will see a number. That number tells you how many people have done that action.

  • 💬 24 — 24 people have replied to this post
  • 🔁 150 — 150 people have reposted it
  • ❤️ 2,400 — 2,400 people have liked it

Verified Checkmarks on X

On X, you may see a colored checkmark next to some usernames. Here is what the colors mean:

IconColorWhat It Means
✅BlueThis account pays for X Premium (a subscription). It does NOT necessarily mean the person is famous or trustworthy — anyone can pay for it.
✅GoldVerified official organization — a business or company.
✅GrayVerified government account or official institution.
⚠️

Important: Blue Checkmarks Are Not What They Used to Be

On the old Twitter, a blue checkmark meant the account was verified as genuinely belonging to a public figure. Now on X, anyone can pay for a blue checkmark. This means a blue checkmark alone is not proof that someone is who they claim to be. Always check the account's history and follower count before trusting news from an unknown blue-checked account.

X Navigation Icons

IconNameWhat It Does
🏠HomeYour main feed, showing posts from accounts you follow plus recommended content.
🔍Search / ExploreSearch for topics, hashtags, or people. See trending topics.
🔔NotificationsShows likes, reposts, new followers, and replies to your posts.
✉️MessagesDirect Messages — private conversations between you and another user.
✏️Compose (pencil / feather)Creates a new post. Usually a floating icon at the bottom right of the screen.
