What Is Reddit?

Reddit is a giant collection of online communities called subreddits. Each subreddit focuses on one topic — cooking, gardening, local news, science, movies, hobbies, and thousands more. Users post content and vote on what is most valuable.

The Most Unique Thing About Reddit: Voting

Reddit's defining feature is a voting system. Every post and every comment has two arrows next to it:

▲
Upvote (Orange Arrow Up)
Tap the up arrow if you think this post or comment is good, helpful, interesting, or valuable. The number goes up.
▼
Downvote (Blue/Gray Arrow Down)
Tap the down arrow if the post or comment is off-topic, unhelpful, or low quality. The number goes down.
1.2k
Vote Score
The number between the arrows = upvotes minus downvotes. Higher scores mean the community found this post valuable.
💡

Why Does Voting Matter?

Posts and comments with more upvotes rise to the top so more people see them. Posts with many downvotes get buried. This is Reddit's way of letting the community decide what is worth reading — no editor or algorithm decides alone.

What Is Karma?

Karma is your Reddit reputation score. Every time someone upvotes your post or comment, you gain karma points. Every time someone downvotes you, you lose a few. Your karma score appears on your profile.

High karma means you have been contributing content that other users appreciate. It has no real-world value — it is just a way to show you are a trusted, active community member.

Icons on Each Reddit Post

IconNameWhat It Does
💬CommentsShows how many comments the post has. Tap it to open all the comments and read the discussion.
🔗ShareCopies the link to this post or opens sharing options so you can send it to someone.
🔖SaveSaves the post to your profile's "Saved" section so you can find it later.
⋮More Options (three dots)Opens additional options: report the post, hide it from your feed, view the poster's profile, or copy link.
🏆AwardGive the poster a special award (some cost real money via Reddit coins). Shows appreciation for exceptional content.

Reddit Navigation Icons

IconNameWhat It Does
🏠HomeYour personalized feed based on the subreddits you have joined.
🔍SearchFind subreddits, posts, or users.
➕Create PostCreate a new post in a subreddit you belong to.
🔔NotificationsAlerts for replies to your comments, mentions, and activity on your posts.
👤ProfileYour Reddit profile showing your posts, comments, and karma score.

Post Flair Icons

Many subreddits use colored labels called flair tags. These appear next to post titles as colored pills or badges. They help you quickly see what type of content a post contains. For example, in a cooking community: [Recipe] [Question] [Review].
