About Facebook

Facebook is one of the world's largest social networks, used by over 3 billion people. It is a place to connect with family and friends, share photos and updates, join community groups, and find local events. The icons on Facebook help you do all of these things.

The Facebook Navigation Bar

At the top of the Facebook app (on your phone) or at the top of the website, there is a row of icons. These are your main navigation — like tabs at the top of a notebook.

IconNameWhat It Does
🏠Home (News Feed)Your main Facebook feed — posts from friends, family, and pages you follow. This is where you start.
👥FriendsShows friend requests you have received, and lets you find people you know to add as friends.
🛍MarketplaceBuy and sell items locally, like a digital classifieds section or garage sale. Similar to Craigslist.
▶️Watch (Videos)Facebook's video section — shows you videos from pages you follow and popular videos others are watching.
👥GroupsCommunities of people who share a common interest. Join groups about hobbies, your town, faith communities, etc.
🔔NotificationsA bell with possibly a red number. Shows all your recent notifications — likes, comments, birthdays, and more.
☰Menu (three lines)Opens your full account menu — your profile, pages, events, settings, and logout.

Post Reactions — More Than Just a "Like"

When you see a post in your Facebook feed, there is a row of icons at the bottom of it. The first one says "Like" with a thumbs-up icon. But if you hold your finger on the Like button for a moment (or hover your mouse over it on a computer), six reaction emojis appear.

👍
Like
A thumbs up. The most basic reaction — you approve of or enjoyed the post.
❤️
Love
A red heart. Stronger than a Like — you love this post or feel warmly about it.
🤣
Haha
A laughing face. The post made you laugh or smile.
😮
Wow
A surprised face. The post surprised or amazed you.
😢
Sad
A sad face. You feel sad about what was shared — condolences, bad news, etc.
😡
Angry
A red angry face. You are upset or angered by something in the post (often used for frustrating news stories).
💡

How to Leave a Reaction

On phone: Press and hold your finger on the "Like" button for about 1 second until the reaction emojis pop up. Then slide your finger to the one you want and let go.
On computer: Hover your mouse over the "Like" button (don't click) and wait a moment for the reactions to appear, then click your choice.

Comment, Share, and Send

Below every Facebook post, there are usually three action options:

IconNameWhat It Does
💬CommentOpens a text box where you can type a response, question, or thought about the post. Others can see your comment.
↩️ShareShares this post to your own profile, to a friend's profile, in a group, or via a private message. You can add your own comment when sharing.
✈️Send (Messenger)Sends the post privately to a friend through Facebook Messenger — only they will see it, not anyone else.

Facebook Messenger Icons

Messenger is Facebook's private messaging system. When you are inside a Messenger conversation, you will see these icons:

IconNameWhat It Does
📷CameraStart a video call with this person.
📞PhoneMake a voice call (audio only).
ℹ️Info (i)See details about this conversation, change notification settings, or report/block the person.
➕Attach / AddAttach a photo, file, location, or GIF to your message.
🎤Voice MessageRecord and send an audio message instead of typing.
👍Quick LikeSend a single thumbs-up emoji quickly as a response.
✓✓Checkmarks (message status)One checkmark = sent. Two checkmarks = delivered. A filled circle with the person's profile picture = read.

Other Important Facebook Icons

IconNameWhat It Does
📅EventsView and RSVP to local events, birthday parties, community gatherings, and more.
⭐SavedItems you have "saved" for later — articles, posts, products.
🚩Report / FlagReport a post or comment that you believe violates Facebook's rules. Use responsibly.
🙈Hide PostRemoves a post from your feed without unfriending the person. You will see fewer posts like it.
📍Check InShare your current location — "I'm at Joe's Diner!" — on a post.
