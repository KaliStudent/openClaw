About Instagram

Instagram is a photo and video sharing platform. It focuses primarily on visuals — photos and short videos. People follow each other to see each other's content. It is owned by the same company as Facebook (Meta).

Instagram Bottom Navigation Bar

Instagram's navigation bar runs along the bottom of the app screen (on mobile). There are five icons in a row.

🏠
Home
Your main feed — posts from accounts you follow appear here in order.

Post Icons — Under Every Photo or Video

When you look at a photo or video on Instagram, you will see a row of icons just below the content on the right side (or bottom).

IconNameWhat It Does
🤍Heart (Like)Tap once to "like" the post — the heart turns red. Double-tapping the photo itself also likes it.
💬Comment (Speech Bubble)Opens a box where you can type a comment. Others will be able to see your comment.
✈️Send / Share (Paper Plane)Send this post to a friend as a direct message (private). Like forwarding an email — only they see it.
🔖Save (Ribbon/Bookmark)Saves the post to your private collection to view later. The person who posted it cannot see that you saved it.
💡

Double-Tap to Like!

On Instagram, you can quickly "like" any photo by double-tapping (tapping it twice quickly) anywhere on the image. A white heart will briefly appear in the center to confirm your like. This is a popular shortcut that many Instagram users love.

Stories — The Colorful Rings

At the very top of your Instagram home screen, you will see a row of circular profile pictures with colorful rings around them. These are called Stories.

  • Colorful ring = This person posted a new story you have not seen yet. Tap to view it.
  • Gray ring = You have already seen this person's story.
  • Tap to view — the story plays for a few seconds, then moves to the next person's story automatically.
  • Stories disappear after 24 hours — they are temporary, like a chalkboard that gets erased.

Instagram Direct Messages (DM)

Direct Messages (usually called "DMs") are private messages between you and another Instagram user. No one else can see them.

To access DMs: tap the paper airplane icon (✈️) in the top right corner of the Home screen, or tap the paper plane icon below any post to send it as a DM.

IconWhat It Means in DMs
📷Take and send a photo or video directly in the message
🖼️Send a photo from your photo library
❤️Quick-react to a message with a heart (press and hold on a message)
🎤Record a voice message instead of typing
✓✓Message checkmarks: one dark check = delivered; two checks = read

Other Instagram Icons Worth Knowing

IconNameLocation & Meaning
➕Create Post / AddA plus sign, often in the bottom center or near the profile area. Tap to create a new post, story, or reel.
⋮More Options (three dots)In the top right corner of any post. Opens options: report, copy link, share, mute, or turn off notifications for that post.
🔵Blue Verified CheckmarkA blue circle with a check. Means this is a verified, authentic account — usually for public figures, brands, or celebrities.
🔔Follow Notification BellOn a profile you follow: tap to turn on post notifications so you are alerted every time they post something new.
