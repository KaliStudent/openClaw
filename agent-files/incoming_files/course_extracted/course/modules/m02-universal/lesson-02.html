Finding Things on a Website

Almost every website and app has a way to search for something — and the icons that go with searching are among the most useful on the internet. Learn these and you will be able to find anything on any website.

🔍
Magnifying Glass (Search)
The universal icon for "Search." Tap it or click the search bar next to it, type what you want to find, and press Enter.
Universal
🎤

Point your camera at something and the app will search for it. Google Lens uses this to identify objects, plants, landmarks, and more.

The Magnifying Glass — The Most Important Search Icon

The magnifying glass 🔍 is the universal sign for "search here." You will find it:

  • In the top right or top center of most websites
  • In the toolbar of apps like Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube
  • On the home screen of your phone (usually at the top)
  • Inside the Google app
💡

Tip: The Search Bar Might Be Hidden

On phones, the full search bar (the text box you type in) is sometimes hidden until you tap the magnifying glass icon. Tap the magnifying glass first, then type.

Voice Search — Type with Your Voice

The microphone icon 🎤 inside a search bar lets you speak your search out loud. This is incredibly useful if:

  • You find typing on a small phone screen difficult
  • You are not sure how to spell something
  • You want to search quickly without stopping what you are doing

Just tap the microphone, wait for the beep or the circle to appear, and say what you want to search for. The phone will type it for you.

Filter and Sort — When You Have Too Many Results

Imagine you search for "blue sweater" on a shopping website and get 2,400 results. That is too many to look through. This is where filter and sort icons come to the rescue.

  • Filter removes results that do not match your requirements (only size Large, only under $40, only 4-star reviews)
  • Sort rearranges the results in a useful order (cheapest first, newest first, best-rated first)

Filter icons often look like a funnel (▽) or sliders. Sort icons often look like two arrows pointing in opposite directions (↕ or ↑↓).

📖 Vocabulary

Search Bar
A text box where you type words to search a website or app. Often has a magnifying glass icon next to it.
Voice Search
Searching by speaking out loud instead of typing. Activated by the microphone icon.
Filter
A tool that limits search results to only show items matching certain criteria (price, color, size, etc.).
Sort
Arranging a list of results in a specific order — such as alphabetically, by price, or by date.
Google Lens
A Google feature that lets you search by pointing your phone's camera at an object. The camera icon in Google activates this.
