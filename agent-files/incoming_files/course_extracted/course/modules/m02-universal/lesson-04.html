Icons That Tell You What's Happening

Status icons do not require you to do anything — they are giving you information. But knowing what they mean helps you make better decisions about what to do next.

🔔
Bell (Notifications)
Shows you have alerts waiting — new messages, replies, likes, or important updates. Tapping it shows them.
Universal
🔕
Bell with Slash (Muted)
Notifications are turned off for this conversation or app. You will not be disturbed.
Universal
🔒
Padlock (Secure / Locked)
In a browser, a closed padlock means the website is secure. Your information is encrypted and protected. This is a good sign.
Web Browsers
⚠️
Warning Triangle
Something needs your attention — an error, a warning about a website, or a problem with a process.
Universal
⏳
Loading / Spinner
A spinning circle or dots. The device is working on something — loading a page, saving a file, or processing a request. Wait for it.
Universal
3
Red Badge Number
A red circle with a number on top of an app icon (like on your phone home screen). It shows how many unread notifications or messages are waiting.
iOS / Android
✅
Checkmark / Done
Something completed successfully — a message sent, a form submitted, a purchase confirmed.
Universal
❌
Error / Failed
Something did not work — a message failed to send, a file did not upload, or a connection was lost.
Universal

The Padlock Icon and Your Online Safety

In your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge), there is a small icon to the left of every website address. This is one of the most important icons for your safety online.

IconWhat It MeansWhat to Do
🔒 Closed padlock — Connection is encrypted and secure Safe to enter passwords or personal information
⚠️ Warning or "Not Secure" label — Connection is not encrypted Do NOT enter passwords or payment information on this site
🚫 Red warning — Browser detected a dangerous or fake website Leave immediately — do not proceed
🛡️

Safety Rule: Always Check for the Padlock Before Paying

Before entering your credit card number or bank information on any website, look for the closed padlock 🔒 in your browser's address bar. If it is missing or shows a warning, do not enter your information.

The Loading Spinner — Patience Is the Key

A spinning circle, spinning dots, or a rotating indicator means your device is working on something. This might be:

  • Loading a webpage (especially on slow internet)
  • Saving or uploading a file
  • Processing a purchase
  • Installing an update
⭐

Do NOT Tap Repeatedly When You See a Spinner

When a spinning circle appears, many people tap the button again thinking nothing happened. This often causes the action to happen twice — like submitting a payment twice. See the spinner? Wait patiently. If it spins for more than 2 minutes, then try refreshing the page.
