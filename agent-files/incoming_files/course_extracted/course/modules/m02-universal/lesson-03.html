Icons That Make Things Happen

Action icons are the "doing" icons. When you tap one, something happens immediately. This lesson covers the most common ones you will see everywhere.

⬆️📤
Share
A box with an upward arrow. Sends a link, photo, or file to someone else — via text, email, or other apps.
iOS / Universal
⬇️
Download
An arrow pointing downward (into a tray or line). Saves a file from the internet onto your device.
Universal
⬆️
Upload
An arrow pointing upward. Sends a file from your device to the internet or another service.
Universal
✏️
Edit (Pencil)
A pencil or pen icon. Lets you change or modify something — like editing your profile information or a comment you wrote.
Universal
🗑️
Delete (Trash)
A trash can or bin. Removes or deletes the selected item. Often asks "Are you sure?" before permanently deleting.
Universal
🔄
Refresh / Reload
A circular arrow. Reloads the current page to show the latest version. Does not delete anything.
Universal
📋
Copy
Two overlapping pages. Copies selected text or content to your "clipboard" so you can paste it elsewhere.
Universal
🔖
Bookmark / Save
A ribbon or flag shape. Saves an item to come back to later — like bookmarking a webpage or saving a post.
Universal
➕
Add / New
A plus sign. Creates something new — a new post, a new contact, a new file, or a new item.
Universal
💾
Save (Floppy Disk)
An old floppy disk image. Saves your current work. Seen in document editors like Word and Google Docs.
Desktop Apps

The Share Icon — One of the Most Useful Things on Your Phone

The Share icon is one of the most underused features on any phone, and once you know it, you will use it constantly.

What it looks like on iPhone: A box with an arrow pointing straight up out of the top.

What it looks like on Android: Three dots connected by two lines, forming a shape that looks like a sideways Y or a connected network.

What happens when you tap Share? A panel slides up from the bottom of your screen showing all the ways you can send the thing. You can:

  • Send it as a text message
  • Send it by email
  • Copy the link to paste it somewhere else
  • Post it to Facebook, Instagram, or another app
  • Save it to your notes or files
💡

Stop Taking Photos of Your Screen!

If you have ever taken a photo of your phone screen to send a webpage or article to someone — there is a much better way. Use the Share icon instead. It sends the actual link, which looks much cleaner and the other person can tap it to open the real page.

Download vs. Upload — What's the Difference?

These two get mixed up constantly. Here is an easy way to remember:

IconWordWhat It MeansExample
⬇️ Download Getting something from the internet onto your device Saving a photo to your phone's photo album
⬆️ Upload Sending something from your device up to the internet Posting a photo you took to Facebook

Memory trick: Down = things falling down onto your device. Up = things going up to the cloud.

The Trash Can Icon — Be Careful, But Don't Be Afraid

The trash can 🗑️ deletes things. Many people are nervous about this icon, and that caution is healthy — but do not let fear stop you from using it.

🛡️

Most Apps Ask Before Deleting

When you tap the trash icon, most apps will show a message like: "Are you sure you want to delete this? This cannot be undone." You must confirm by tapping "Delete" or "Yes." If you see that message and you are unsure, just tap "Cancel."
