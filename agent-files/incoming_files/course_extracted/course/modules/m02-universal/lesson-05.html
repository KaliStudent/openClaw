Your Account and Your Settings

Every website and app that you log into has a place to manage your account and a place to change settings. Knowing the icons that lead to these areas puts you in control of your entire digital experience.

👤
Person / Profile
An outline of a person or a circle with initials. Opens your account, profile page, or account management area.
Universal
⚙️
Gear / Settings
A cogwheel or gear shape. Opens the settings panel where you can change how the app or website works.
Universal
🔑
Sign In / Log In
A key, a person with an arrow, or simply "Sign In" text. Opens the login page where you enter your email and password.
Universal
→🚪
Sign Out / Log Out
A person with an arrow pointing away, or a door with an arrow. Signs you out of your account safely.
Universal
🔒
Privacy / Security
A padlock or shield inside a settings area. Controls who can see your information and how your account is protected.
Universal
🌙
Dark Mode / Night Mode
A moon or half-sun/half-moon icon. Switches the screen to dark colors — easier on the eyes at night.
Universal
🔤
Text Size / Accessibility
An "A" with arrows or two differently sized A's. Opens options to make text larger, change contrast, or enable assistive features.
Universal

The Settings Gear — Your Control Center

The gear icon ⚙️ is one of the most powerful icons on your device because it leads to settings — a place where you can customize everything to work the way you want it to.

Inside the settings of most apps and websites you can:

  • Change your password
  • Make text bigger (accessibility settings)
  • Control who sees your posts (privacy settings)
  • Turn notifications on or off
  • Change the language
  • Switch to dark mode (easier on the eyes)
💡

Hidden Feature: Make Text Bigger

If text on your phone is hard to read, go to Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size on iPhone, or Settings → Accessibility → Font Size on Android. There is a slider you can move to make ALL text on your phone larger instantly.

Sign Out Safely

When you use a shared computer — at a library, a doctor's office, or a friend's house — it is very important to sign out when you are done. If you do not sign out, the next person who uses that computer may have access to your account.

To sign out: tap the person icon 👤 (usually in the top right corner), then look for "Sign Out," "Log Out," or a door-with-arrow icon.

🛡️

Always Sign Out on Shared Computers

On your own personal phone or home computer, you do not need to sign out every time. But on any public or shared device, always sign out before you leave.
