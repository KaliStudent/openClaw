Icons That Help You Move Around

Navigation icons are the icons that help you get from one place to another — like a map and compass for websites. Once you know these, you will never feel lost on any website again.

The Hamburger Menu — In Depth

The three-line "hamburger" icon ☰ is one of the most important icons on the internet, and one of the least understood by people new to technology.

It is called a hamburger menu because the three lines look like a bun-patty-bun sandwich when you squint a little. Designers came up with this nickname, and it stuck.

What happens when you tap the hamburger menu? A panel slides in from the left (or sometimes a list drops down) showing all the main sections of the website or app. Think of it as opening a table of contents.

📖

Why Is the Menu Hidden?

On phone screens, space is limited. Designers "hide" navigation links behind the hamburger icon to keep the screen uncluttered. On larger computer screens, the menu is often visible without needing to tap anything.

The Back Arrow — Your Best Friend

The back arrow ← is the single most used navigation icon in the world. It simply takes you to where you just were.

On an iPhone: The back arrow is usually in the top left corner of the screen, often with a label next to it like "Back" or the name of the previous page.
On Android: There is a back button built into the phone (either a physical button or a virtual one on screen).
On a computer: The back arrow is in the top left corner of your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

💡

Tapping Back Will Never Delete Anything

Many people are afraid to tap the back arrow because they think they will lose something. You will not. The back arrow simply rewinds to the previous screen — like rewinding a video.

Breadcrumbs — Know Exactly Where You Are

Breadcrumbs are a row of clickable links near the top of many websites that show you a trail of where you have been. They look like this:

The name comes from the fairy tale Hansel and Gretel, where children dropped bread crumbs to find their way home through the forest. Digital breadcrumbs do the same thing — they show you the path you took to get to the current page and let you click any step to go back there.

🎯

Practice Activity

Open your internet browser and go to any website. Look for:
1. A hamburger menu icon (☰) — usually in the top left or top right
2. A back arrow — top left of the browser
3. An X to close something
Tap each one to see what it does. You can always hit Back to undo.

📖 Vocabulary

Hamburger Menu (☰)
Three horizontal lines icon. Tapping it opens the main navigation menu of an app or website. Named for its resemblance to a hamburger.
Navigation
Moving between pages, sections, or screens within a website or app.
Back Arrow (←)
An icon that takes you back to the screen or page you were just looking at.
Breadcrumbs
A row of links near the top of a page showing where you are in a website's structure (e.g., Home > Shop > Shoes).
Kebab Menu (⋮)
Three vertical dots icon. Opens a list of additional options. Common on Android and web apps.
Ellipsis (…)
Three dots in a row (horizontal). On iOS and many websites, tapping this shows more options.
