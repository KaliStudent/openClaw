How to Practice What You Learn

Reading about icons is good. Actually using them is even better. Here is a simple practice method called "Spot It, Name It, Use It."

📱

The "Spot It, Name It, Use It" Method

Step 1 — SPOT IT: After a lesson, pick up your phone or open a website and look for the icons you just learned about.
Step 2 — NAME IT: Out loud (or in your head), say what the icon is called and what it does.
Step 3 — USE IT: Actually tap or click the icon to see what happens. You cannot break anything.

⭐

You Cannot Break Anything by Tapping

One of the biggest fears people have is that they will tap the wrong thing and break their phone or delete everything. In almost every case, you can undo anything you do accidentally, and exploring icons will not damage your device. When in doubt, you can always close the app and start over.

A Note About Differences Between Devices

One thing that can be confusing is that the same icon can look slightly different on different phones and websites. Apple's version of a share icon looks a little different from Google's. This is normal.

In this course, we will show you the most common version of each icon, and we will always tell you when there are important differences to watch for.
